## 📊 Project Title:
HR Analytics Dashboard – Employee Attrition Insights (Power BI)


## 📝 Project Overview:  
This project presents an **interactive HR Analytics Dashboard** built using Power BI to analyze employee attrition and workforce trends.  
It transforms raw HR data into actionable insights, helping organizations improve employee retention and decision-making.


## 🎯 Purpose:  
- Analyze employee attrition patterns  
- Identify factors affecting employee turnover  
- Monitor workforce metrics (age, salary, experience)  
- Enable data-driven HR decision-making


## 🛠️ Tech Stack:  
- 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard creation  
- 🔄 **Power Query** – Data cleaning & transformation  
- 📐 **DAX** – Measures & KPI calculations  
- 📁 **Excel / CSV** – Data handling


## 📂 Data Source:  
- HR dataset containing employee details (age, salary, job role, education, attrition)  
- Learned and implemented via a **YouTube Power BI tutorial (project-based learning)**


## 🚀 Features & Highlights:  

### 🔍 Business Problem:
Organizations struggle to understand **why employees leave**, leading to high attrition costs and poor workforce planning.

### 🎯 Goal: 
To build an **interactive dashboard** that provides insights into employee attrition and helps improve retention strategies.

### 📈 Key Metrics:
- 👥 Total Employees: **1470**  
- ❌ Attrition Count: **237**  
- 📉 Attrition Rate: **16.1%**  
- 🎂 Average Age: **37**  
- 💰 Average Salary: **6.5K**  
- ⏳ Avg Years at Company: **7**

### 📊 Key Visuals:
- 📌 KPI Cards for quick insights  
- 🍩 Attrition by Education (Donut Chart)  
- 📊 Attrition by Job Role (Bar Chart)  
- 💰 Attrition by Salary Slab  
- 📉 Attrition by Years at Company (Line Chart)  
- 👥 Attrition by Age Group  
- 📋 Job Role Breakdown Matrix  
- 🎛️ Department Filters (HR, R&D, Sales)

### ⭐ Key Features:  
- Interactive and dynamic filtering  
- Multi-dimensional attrition analysis  
- Identification of high-risk employee groups  
- Clean and professional dashboard UI  
- Insightful and easy-to-understand visuals


## 💡 Key Insights:  
- 🔹 Highest attrition in **age group 26–35**  
- 🔹 Employees earning **≤ 5K salary** show highest attrition  
- 🔹 Roles like **Laboratory Technician & Sales Executive** have high turnover  
- 🔹 Employees in **early years of employment** are more likely to leave
